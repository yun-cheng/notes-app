Privacy

Notes · last changed November 19, 2026

Notes is a personal notes and todos app. This page says what it keeps about you, where it keeps it, and who can see it. It has no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and nothing you put in it is sold or handed to anyone.

Your account

You sign in with Google. From Google the app gets your name, email address, profile photo address and Google account ID, and keeps them to know it's you and to show who wrote what to the people you share with. A single cookie keeps you signed in.

Your notes

  • Your notes, todos, comments, reactions, tags and each note's earlier versions are kept on the app's server, run on Cloudflare, so they reach your other devices, and in your browser, so they work offline.
  • A vault you lock with a PIN is encrypted on your device before it's saved. The server only ever holds it encrypted and can't read it.
  • Other vaults are stored as you write them, on Cloudflare's encrypted storage, so the server can read them to sync them and to show them to people you share them with.

Photos and Google Drive

If you connect Google Drive, photos you add are shrunk on your device and saved in your own Drive, in a "Notes app photos" folder. The app asks only for access to files it creates itself (drive.file), so it can't see anything else in your Drive. The server keeps the access Google gives it, to save photos and show them to you and to people in a vault you share. Disconnecting Drive in Settings gives that access back to Google; the photos stay in your Drive.

The app's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements: it is used only to provide these features to you.

What leaves the app, and when

  • Translation: when you translate an entry, its text is translated on Cloudflare (Workers AI). An entry in a locked vault never goes there: it's translated on your device, if your browser can. When neither works, the menu offers Google Translate, which sends the text to Google only if you pick it.
  • Link previews and pasted pictures: the server fetches the web pages you link to and pictures you paste by address, and a preview's image loads from the linked site. Links in a locked vault are never previewed.
  • Reminders: if you turn them on, reminders reach you through your browser's push service.
  • AI apps: an AI app such as Claude can read or change your notes only if you connect it, and only in the vaults you choose. You can disconnect it in Settings.

Sharing

A vault or note you share can be seen by the people you share it with, with their name, email and photo shown to each other. A public link can be opened by anyone who has it, until it expires or you turn it off.

Taking your notes with you, or away

Settings can export all your notes as Markdown at any time. A note you delete goes to the Trash, where you can restore it; it and its earlier versions stay on the server until your account is deleted. Delete account, at the end of Settings, deletes your account and everything in it from the server at once: your vaults and their entries, history, comments and links (people you shared a vault with lose it too), your comments in other people's vaults, and your sessions and connections. What you wrote in someone else's vault stays theirs, without your name. Your photos stay in your Google Drive for you to keep or delete, and the app's access to it ends. The app's copy on the device you delete from is removed too. Your other devices are signed out, but their copy stays in that browser until you clear its data for this site or uninstall the app there.
